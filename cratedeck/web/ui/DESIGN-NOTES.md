# CrateDeck Onboarding — Design Notes

**Status:** 📚 REFERENCE — implementation-facing onboarding baseline.

- Audience: a DJ opening CrateDeck for the first time before trusting a drive at a gig.
- One action: plug in a drive and follow the scan → verify → preflight path.
- Five-second message: CrateDeck finds the USB failures that can ruin a set and names the fix.
- Steps today: 1) orient 2) find the surface 3) understand the job 4) act.
- Steps after: the same four-step path; every explanatory label remains secondary but becomes legible, with no extra click or field to remove.
